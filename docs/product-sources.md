## Images
`public/images/products/<slug>/main.webp` is the manufacturer's white-background studio shot, trimmed and fitted on a
4:3 white canvas. `gallery-N.webp` are further official shots (angles and details), cropped to 4:3.
Nothing was edited beyond crop, scale and compression.

## Left out on purpose
- Real-world range (km) and weights the manufacturer does not publish (Haibike, Longtail).
- Models whose official page offered fewer than three photos (Haibike Trekking 3, Trekking 7.5 Bosch, Alltrack 4).
- No Shimano, Brose or Bafang motor models, and no models under €2,499, because none could be sourced
  from a manufacturer page that allowed access.
